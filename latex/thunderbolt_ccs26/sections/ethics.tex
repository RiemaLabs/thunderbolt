\section{Ethical Considerations}
Our work studies consensus-critical serialization logic in real-world clients, which raises the risk that discovered behaviors could be exploited to trigger inconsistent state roots across implementations. We mitigate this risk by performing all experiments offline in controlled environments, without interacting with live networks or user data. All vulnerabilities discovered in this work were handled under responsible disclosure and were reported to the relevant teams before the paper was submitted. After confirming each issue, the authors sent private reports through the projects' official security contacts. The reports included technical descriptions and the minimal proof-of-concept needed to demonstrate the problem. Every affected maintainer acknowledged receipt or confirmed that a patch or mitigation was in place before submission.
