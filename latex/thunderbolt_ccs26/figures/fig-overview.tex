\caption{Illustrative overview of \thunderbolt. A Bitcoin UTXO is locked once on-chain under the fixed key $P = (x_s + y_s)G$ at \textsf{Setup}, and the chain sees no further activity until \textsf{Finalize}. Ownership transfers between successive holders $\mathsf{H}_0, \mathsf{H}_1, \ldots$ happen entirely off-chain (dashed box). At each step, a fresh refresh value $r_s$ is added to the current holder's secret and subtracted from the Committee share, so that $(x_s + y_s)G$ remains equal to $P$ throughout.}
\label{fig:overview}
\Description{Diagram showing the Thunderbolt protocol overview: a Bitcoin chain strip at top with Setup and Finalize events, a horizontal chain of holders H0 through H4 connected by off-chain transfer arrows in the middle, and a Committee node centered below receiving subtract-r updates from each holder.}
