# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Bitcoin Thunderbolt open and respectful for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or technical preference.

We will act and interact in ways that support an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful in technical disagreement.
- Give specific, actionable feedback and assume honest intent when the facts allow it.
- Acknowledge mistakes, correct them, and credit other people's work.
- Keep reviews focused on the change, evidence, and documented project goals.
- Respect privacy and responsible disclosure.
- Accept a maintainer's decision to pause a discussion that has become harmful or unproductive.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language or imagery and unwanted sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing another person's private information without permission
- Threats, intimidation, stalking, or sustained disruption
- Pressuring someone to disclose credentials, identity, employment, or personal details
- Posting a suspected vulnerability publicly after being directed to private reporting
- Conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This code applies in repositories, issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, private project communication, events, and other spaces where someone represents Bitcoin Thunderbolt.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's "Report a vulnerability" form on the Security tab and identify the report as a code-of-conduct concern. GitHub private reporting must be enabled before the repository is public.

Do not ask the subject of a report to handle it. If a project maintainer is involved, state that in the private report so an uninvolved maintainer can respond.

Maintainers will protect the reporter's privacy as far as practical, collect only information needed to assess the incident, and avoid retaliation. False reports made in bad faith may themselves violate this code.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take any action they consider appropriate and proportionate, including a private correction, warning, content removal, temporary restriction, or permanent ban from project spaces. Serious threats or illegal conduct may be referred to the relevant platform or authorities.

Enforcement decisions should state the violated rule and expected corrective action. An appeal goes to two uninvolved maintainers, whose decision is final for project spaces.
